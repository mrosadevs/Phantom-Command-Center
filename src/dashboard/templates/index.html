Heartbeat System
How Phantom thinks proactively without being asked
Every 30 minutes, Phantom reads memory/HEARTBEAT.md — a checklist of things to monitor. It asks LM Studio: "is there anything worth telling Theodore right now?"
HEARTBEAT_OK
Nothing matters → completely silent, no DM
Real signal
DMs you directly — queue backed up, high-priority backlog, pattern detected, etc.
Monitors:
📋 Research queue · 🔨 Backlog priorities · 🧠 Memory integrity · 🔁 Repeated patterns · ⚠️ Unresolved errors
💡 Edit memory/HEARTBEAT.md to change what Phantom watches for — it's plain Markdown.
